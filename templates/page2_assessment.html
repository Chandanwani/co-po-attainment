Level points: 2.0 to 2.9 (2 + (% - 70)/10)

Level points: 1.0 to 1.9 (1 + (% - 60)/10)
Level 0-1: 0% to <60% students scoring ≥50%
Level points: 0.0 to 1.0 ((% × 1)/60, not fixed to 0)
Exact Points Lookup Table:
60% → 1.0 61% → 1.1 65% → 1.5 67% → 1.7 69% → 1.9 70% → 2.0 71% → 2.1 75% → 2.5 79% → 2.9 ≥80% → 3.0
Example: For 35% students passing → Attainment = (35 × 1) / 60 = 0.58 → Level: Level 0-1 (0.58).
